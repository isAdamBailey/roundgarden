# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Three.js + Vite, confirmed by the user.

## Users

Players using a desktop or mobile browser. The first build is a casual, immersive 360-degree game.

## Product Purpose

Let the player float at the center of a sphere, look in every direction, and catch objects as they drift within reach. Success is measured by the number caught during a short round.

## Positioning

The player explores a full spherical environment from its center and catches approaching objects by aiming and reaching at close range.

## Operating Context

Played in a modern browser on desktop or mobile. Mouse or touch controls aim and catch. Camera based hand tracking is not part of the first build.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Render the 3D game using WebGL through Three.js.
- Objects visibly increase in apparent size as they approach.
- The first version uses mouse and touch input.
- Catching is limited to nearby objects within the player's aiming reach.
- WebXR and camera-based hand tracking are undecided future options.

## Brand Commitments

The user refined the world to a lush greenhouse in space with whimsical vegetables, curling stems, flowers, and dense vines.

## Evidence on Hand

No existing assets, logo, or product evidence was supplied.

## Product Principles

- Keep the player floating at the center of a complete, explorable sphere.
- Make distance and reach understandable through object scale and motion.
- Keep catching immediate and comfortable with mouse or touch.
