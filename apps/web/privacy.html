Privacy in
Roundgarden.

The games do not collect personal data. Visiting the website is different: the web host and services used to deliver the page may receive basic connection information.

The iPhone, iPad, and Mac apps

The Roundgarden apps do not collect, transmit, or share personal data. They do not include analytics, advertising, accounts, or crash-reporting services. A personal-best score is saved on your device only, using the platform’s local app storage. The apps do not send that score to us.

Playing and browsing on the web

The browser game saves your personal-best score in local browser storage on your device. It does not send the score to us. The website does not currently use analytics, advertising, account sign-in, or a contact form.

When your browser requests a page or game file, the web hosting service may process connection details such as your IP address, browser information, request time, and requested file. The host’s server and security logs depend on the hosting configuration.

Services that deliver page content

The landing page loads its display and text fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google to retrieve those font files, so Google may receive connection information such as your IP address and browser details. If official store badges are enabled, your browser also requests badge artwork from Apple or Google Play. Following a store badge takes you to that store, where its own privacy policy applies.

Your choices

You can clear or block local browser storage through your browser settings; the browser game remains playable, but its personal-best score may not be saved. You can also block external font requests; the site will use its built-in font fallbacks.
